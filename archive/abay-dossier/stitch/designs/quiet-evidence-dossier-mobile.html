Methods & Limitations

This simulation relies on heuristic traffic demand models rather than real-time induction loop data. The emission proxies are calculated using standard HBEFA models without local fleet composition adjustment.

Results indicate potential flow improvement, but funding should be contingent on a limited real-world pilot to validate these synthetic findings.
