Methods & Limitations

This dossier presents findings based on a proxy simulation environment. The data utilized represents a demo configuration relying heavily on static geometric inputs and generic kinematic assumptions, rather than a live telemetry feed.

The non-live real-data snapshot employed for road geometry provides a structural baseline but lacks the dynamic temporal fidelity required for definitive municipal investment decisions. The observed reduction in cycle times from 36 seconds to 32 seconds is mathematically sound within the closed system but remains unvalidated against real-world friction factors.
